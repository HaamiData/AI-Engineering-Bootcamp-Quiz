# Question 9: Checking an Average Response Time Calculation

**Topics:** Computing the Mean, Verifying AI Assistant Output, Arithmetic Errors

## 📝 Question

> We asked an AI assistant to compute the average server response time. The assistant's answer: “The response times of four requests were 200, 300, 100 and 400 milliseconds. The sum of these four numbers is 1000 milliseconds, and dividing by 3 gives an average of about 333 milliseconds.” Which statement about this answer is correct?

## 🔢 Options

**Option 1:** The assistant's calculation is correct and 333 milliseconds is the correct average of this data.

**Option 2:** The mean has no meaning for response time and only the median should be used.

**Option 3:** The sum is wrong and should be 1200; dividing by 3 is correct.

**Option 4:** The sum is correct, but it should be divided by the number of requests (4); the correct average is 250 milliseconds.

## 💡 About this question

This question tests the ability to **verify an AI assistant's output**. The assistant's answer is fluent and confident, but its calculation contains a simple error.

Check: the sum `200 + 300 + 100 + 400 = 1000` is correct. The mean is the sum divided by the **number of data points**; there are 4 requests, not 3. So `1000 / 4 = 250` milliseconds.

## 🔍 Detailed review of every option

### Option 1 — ❌ Incorrect

> The assistant's calculation is correct and 333 milliseconds is the correct average of this data.

**Incorrect.** Dividing by 3 is wrong; there are four data points. 333 ms is the result of an incorrect division. An answer being fluent and confident is not evidence that it is right, and calculations must be verified.

### Option 2 — ❌ Incorrect

> The mean has no meaning for response time and only the median should be used.

**Incorrect.** The mean is perfectly meaningful for response time and is a common metric. (In practice, percentiles such as P95 and P99 are also reported for latency because of its long-tailed distribution, but that does not make the mean meaningless or mean "median only.") Besides, the question asked for the "average," and the flaw in the assistant's answer is an arithmetic error.

### Option 3 — ❌ Incorrect

> The sum is wrong and should be 1200; dividing by 3 is correct.

**Incorrect.** The sum is correct: `200+300+100+400 = 1000`, not 1200. Dividing by 3 is also wrong.

### Option 4 — ✅ Correct

> The sum is correct, but it should be divided by the number of requests (4); the correct average is 250 milliseconds.

**Correct.** The sum (1000) is right, but it must be divided by the number of requests (4): `1000 / 4 = 250` ms. The assistant's mistake is only in the denominator.

## ✅ Correct answer

**Option 4**

> The sum is correct, but it should be divided by the number of requests (4); the correct average is 250 milliseconds.

## 🎯 Why it is correct

Mean = sum ÷ number of data points. The sum is 1000 and the count is 4, so 250 ms. The assistant divided by 3 instead of 4.

## 📌 Key takeaway

Recompute numerical outputs from an AI assistant, even when they sound confident.
